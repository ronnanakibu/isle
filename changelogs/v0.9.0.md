# Release v0.9.0 — Authentic Nusa Mechanics, Full 10-Tab Suite, & Production Polish

### Highlights
- **Dynamic Breathing Lyric Pill (Compact Notch)**:
  - Living karaoke ticker that expands and contracts in real time to hug the length of the currently playing lyric line with physics-based spring elasticity.
  - Smooth slide-up and fade transitions on line change.
  - Intelligent instrumental break detection that cleanly collapses the pill back to minimal resting width displaying the vinyl spinning art, track marquee, and equalizers.
  - Dedicated "Live Lyric Ticker in Notch" preference toggle.

- **Minimalist Navigation Dock & Mouse-Wheel Tab Switcher**:
  - Replaced permanent tab headers with subtle, minimalist white pagination dots at the bottom of the expanded card.
  - Interactive hover expansion morphs the dots into a floating frosted glass icon dock wrapping all 10 activity modules and settings.
  - Fluid mouse-wheel flipping across the entire card flips sequentially between all 10 activity modules with spring sliding transitions and tactile audio clicks.

- **Spotlight Omnibar**:
  - Instant launcher triggered via `Ctrl + K` or `Cmd + K`.
  - Rapid search for songs and synced lyrics, math calculations, currency conversions, and quick navigation actions.

- **Live Synced Lyrics & Audio Spectrum Visualizer**:
  - Direct live lyric fetching with exact match and fallback search.
  - Interactive karaoke viewport with auto-centering and click-to-seek timestamp jumping.
  - Real Web Audio spectrum visualizer with both 3-bar compact pill and 12-bar expanded studio spectrum modes.
  - Local audio file loader supporting `.mp3` and `.wav` tracks for real-time frequency analysis.

- **Shiba Mascot Attention & Etiquette Engine**:
  - 5 expressive vector states: Idle, Low Battery, Charging, Music Vibing, and Happy/Petted.
  - Automatic fullscreen detection smoothly hides the island to ensure zero obstruction during games or movies.
  - Configurable Deep Work / Focus hours and Zen Mode with quiet silhouettes.
  - Smart health nudges for hydration and 20-20-20 eye strain breaks.

- **Tactile Procedural Audio SFX**:
  - Pure synthesizer-based micro-sounds: crisp pops on island expansion/collapse, subtle clicks on tab changes, water droplet splashes on logging drinks, and Tibetan singing bowl chimes on Pomodoro completion.

- **Production Local Data Store**:
  - Structured storage with automatic midnight rollover and streak tracking for hydration.
  - Persistent task manager with priority categorization and clear-completed action.
  - File staging shelf and persistent settings synchronization.
