# Isle v1.1.0 — Live Media Hook, Gestures & System Tray

## What's New

### 🎵 Live System Media Hook
- **Universal Player Sync**: Seamlessly syncs track title, artist name, and live album artwork with active players across Windows (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and media browsers).
- **Direct Control**: Native play/pause, next track, and previous track buttons target the currently active audio session.
- **Dynamic Equalizer**: Equalizer animation pulses in sync with your music and gracefully settles when paused.

### 👆 Touch & Mouse Drag Pager
- **Fluid Horizontal Swipes**: Drag or flick left and right with natural momentum to effortlessly glide between Live Activities.
- **Rubber-Band Resistance**: Authentic Apple-style boundary bounce when swiping past the first or last activity.
- **Interactive Button Highlights**: Smooth glass glow highlights when hovering over playback buttons, interval pills, and checklist tasks.

### ⚙️ System Tray & Display Customization
- **System Tray Icon**: Quick-access tray menu right from your Windows taskbar.
- **Notch Mode vs Floating Mode**:
  - *Notch Mode*: Anchors directly to the physical top bezel with a flush top edge.
  - *Floating Mode*: Floats 10px below the display edge with a rounded pill contour and top specular highlight sheen.
- **Multi-Monitor Support**: Switch Isle instantly between your primary and secondary displays.
- **Run on Windows Startup**: Toggle automatic launch on system boot directly from the tray menu.
