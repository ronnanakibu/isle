# Release v2.0.0 — Zero AI-Slop, Authentic Apple/Nusa Mechanics, & Code Optimization

### Highlights

- **Anti-Slop Visual Purge & Authentic Apple HIG Standard**:
  - Completely purged all radioactive neon glows, exaggerated box-shadows, and neon text shadows across all HUDs, compact pills, and cards.
  - Standardized on OLED pitch-black surfaces with clean 1px specular highlights (`border-white/10`) and Apple system accents (Emerald `#34C759`, Amber `#FF9500`, System Blue `#007AFF`, System Red `#FF3B30`).
  - Refined Zen Breathing and active states with soft, ambient radial blooms (`opacity: 0.15`), eliminating sharp, artificial strokes.

- **Claude Code & Terminal Agent Monitor (Official Logos, No Robot Emojis)**:
  - Replaced generic robot emojis with authentic minimalist developer logos:
    - **Claude Code**: Official Anthropic Spark / Starburst emblem rendered in warm coral.
    - **OpenAI Codex**: Minimalist spiral glyph in clean system green.
    - **Generic CLI Agents**: Crisp terminal glyph.
  - Clean status typography reflecting real CLI activity without tacky neon badges or artificial shadows.

- **Dynamic Sensor Privacy Indicator (Mic, Camera, Location)**:
  - Microphone and camera glyphs are no longer permanently pinned to the compact pill.
  - **Dynamic Sensor Activity**:
    - When an application accesses microphone, camera, or location, Isle smoothly expands with spring physics into a temporary 2.5-second pill toast: `[ 🎙️ Mic in use · Zoom ]` or `[ 📷 Camera Active · Teams ]`.
    - Neighboring elements (clock and mascot) fluidly adjust to make room.
    - After 2.5 seconds, the toast smoothly contracts into a quiet micro-dot (emerald green for Camera, amber for Mic, blue for Location) resting beside the notch.
    - When the app releases the sensor, the indicator glides up and disappears into the bezel completely.

- **Authentic Apple Battery Sweep Fill (Nusa & Apple HIG Parity)**:
  - Removed the glowing green border under the notch.
  - Implemented Apple-style battery sweep fill:
    - Connecting the charger smoothly expands the notch into a horizontal battery card.
    - An Apple battery glyph animates a fluid sweep from 0% up to the current charge percentage, accompanied by clean white typography and an emerald green lightning bolt (`#34C759`).
    - Disconnecting displays a clean unplugged status before smoothly returning to the resting compact pill.

- **Caps Lock & Num Lock Toast Overhaul**:
  - Removed crude nested grey capsule boxes (`[ ⇧ CAPS LOCK OFF ]`).
  - Implemented transient Apple-grade HUD:
    - On **Caps Lock ON**: Upward slide of the `⇧` symbol with clean white typography `Caps Lock` and a solid green status indicator dot.
    - On **Caps Lock OFF**: The `⇧` symbol plays a clean animated diagonal slash (`⇪̸`) and dissolves upwards cleanly.

- **Volume & Brightness Slider: Over-Scroll "Wall Bounce" Physics**:
  - When scrolling or adjusting volume/brightness beyond 100% or 0%, the slider executes an authentic Apple boundary wall bounce / elastic squish, giving tactile physical confirmation that the limit has been reached.

- **Screenshot Auto-Hide Toggle (System Tray & Settings)**:
  - Added a user preference in Settings and developer bar: *"Auto-Hide Isle during Screenshots (PrtSc)"*.
    - **Option A (Hide — Default)**: Isle becomes 100% transparent and tucks away during capture, ensuring it never blocks underlying screen content.
    - **Option B (Stay Visible)**: Isle stays visible on top during capture for easy showcasing and sharing.

- **ThinkPad F4 & Global Mic-Mute Integration**:
  - Global microphone mute (including ThinkPad hardware `F4` hotkey) visually covers Shiba's mouth with clean white cross-tape `( · 🗙 · )`.
  - Unmuting removes the tape and restores the friendly smile `( · ᴥ · )`.
  - Clicking Shiba while muted toggles instant unmute.

- **Media Tab Polish & Fluid Layout**:
  - Completely removed custom music upload buttons, hidden file inputs, and local audio handlers in favor of pure OS media control.
  - Removed harsh vertical divider lines between playback controls and the lyrics pane.
  - Fluid centered layout with a floating glass synced lyrics pane featuring vertical fade masks and strict scroll trapping.

- **Performance & Battery Optimization**:
  - Pruned unused files and dangling imports.
  - Implemented debounced write-behind persistence for all configuration and state stores.
  - Guaranteed 0% idle CPU for animation loops and equalizers when the island is collapsed or inactive.
